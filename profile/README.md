## Trifectum Software

TrifectumSoftware is a game development, general software, hardware, and MC modding group made by iris-lgtm. 

There is no strict theme to the software being made, it can be games, game mods, or practically anything. It is a hub for a few enthusiasts to follow their hobbies, at this point in time.

Discord: https://discord.gg/SSdhf43GDR

## Policies on Projects and joining:

Anyone is free to join TrifectumSoftware or edit any of its open (MIT) projects. Most projects are MIT unless inheriting GPL in-case of forks.
This applies to all projects, including games, software, etc.

## Planned big projects:

- Euler
Multiplatform mod library, with modules for ImGuiMC.

- Trifecta
Groovyscript based scripting mod for 1.7.10, powered by Euler-1.7

- NTM IQ Edition
Fork of NTM with extra content.



## Personal repos

For any personal projects meant for collaborative development, please put them under the trifectumsoftware org.

## AI Usage

AI is permitted to be used for contributions by anyone. This decision is not up for debate. There is some nuance, though;
- Random PRs that change thousands of lines are not to be accepted.
- **NO** AI-Generated assets or copy must be used, with procedural things like fluids being allowed
- You are allowed to use your coding agent of choice (DeepSeek, Claude, etc.)
- Do your best, because all of the AI code is under scrutiny both by the public eye and when maintainers scrutinize it.
- Not using AI isn't an excuse for shitty coding. Learn if you don't know how to do something, whether by AI or by someone else. It's up to you to learn, not the maintainer.

What I (iris-lgtm) have to say about AI is:
I really don't care whether you like it or not. Use it or don't, it's each to one's end. I don't have the time or desire to police people into not using AI, but i will comment on things that look low-quality.
If you don't put effort or thought into your stuff, it shows, and i will scrutinize it no matter if it is AI or not.
On the flip side, if someone has used AI in a way that appears high effort and the person knows what they have done, then it's allowed without any issues. I am not here to scrutinize you on your life choices.
And please, do not constantly harass people for using or not using AI. I've seen this too many times and it tires me.
